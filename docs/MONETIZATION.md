# Monetization

## Current Direction

The existing QR product remains the entry point.

Future monetization can expand from one-time/basic Pro functionality into recurring workflow and developer usage.

## Conceptual Plans

### Free
Basic QR generation and limited workflows.

### Pro
Bulk generation, import/export/print, templates and advanced individual use.

### Workflow
Dynamic QR, workflow configuration, custom fields and hosted workflow UI.

### Developer
API/SDK access, usage limits and integration capabilities.

### Business
Higher API/workflow usage, teams, permissions, analytics, branding and advanced integrations.

## Principle

Do not prematurely lock pricing or quotas in the Core. Product pricing should be configuration/policy around usage capabilities.
